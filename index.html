    Placement rules used

    State routes (IH, US, SH, FM, Loop…)

    No signs anywhere in TxDOT right-of-way, including on poles, trees and traffic signs. Use private frontage behind the right-of-way line, with the owner's OK.

    TxDOT Right of Way Division, campaign signs

    City of San Antonio

    Political signs may stand in the right-of-way of City-maintained streets and stay up to 90 days after the election. Stake them in the ground; never on poles, trees, fences or signs; never in the 25-ft corner triangle or a driver's sight line. Private property needs the owner's written permission. Questions: Chief Sign Inspector, 210-207-0158.

    SA Code Enforcement; Code Ch. 28 §§28-6, 28-10, 28-41, 28-75; Transp. Code §393.0025

    Polling places

    No signs or electioneering within 100 ft of an outside door voters use, from polls opening until the last voter has voted.

    Tex. Elec. Code §61.003

    Private property, statewide

    With the owner's consent, a city may not ban, permit, charge for or size-limit a mainly political sign up to 36 sq ft and 8 ft tall, unlit and without moving parts. County-road right-of-way outside cities is banned ($500–$1,000 a day). HOAs: §259.002.

    Tex. Elec. Code §259.003; Transp. Code §§393.002, 393.007

    Other cities in Bexar County (Selma, Schertz, Converse, Leon Valley…) and unincorporated areas have their own ordinances. This is a planning aid, not legal advice.
